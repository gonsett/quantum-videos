WORK STOPPED
The longest road on Earth is
missing a hundred kilometers.
The Pan-American Highway runs
about thirty thousand kilometers,
from Alaska to the bottom of Argentina.
Except here.
So why has nobody ever finished it?
In Panama,
the road stops at a small town called Yaviza.
It starts again in Colombia.
In between lies a jungle that
no road has ever crossed.
In 1960,
a Land Rover and a Jeep tried it.
It took them more than four months.
In 1971,
the United States agreed to pay two
thirds of the cost of finishing it.
Then,
in 1975,
a federal judge in Washington
ordered the work to stop.
Part of the reason was cows.
South America had foot-and-mouth disease,
and the only thing standing between it
and the herds up north was the Darién.
The road never came.
The people did.
In 2023,
more than five hundred and twenty
thousand walked through it.
On foot.
About one in five was a child.
A jungle that stopped a highway,
but not half a million people.
The full story is on the channel.
