NO RESCUE
True stories of survival.
Archive: Wikimedia Commons (CC BY / CC BY-SA, authors in the description) · Music: original score
She fell three kilometers
out of the sky,
still strapped to her seat.
She was seventeen.
And she survived the fall.
But nobody was coming.
So how do you walk out
of the Amazon alone?
Christmas Eve,
1971.
Lightning strikes LANSA Flight 508,
and the plane breaks apart around her.
She wakes up on the forest floor.
Collarbone broken.
One eye swollen shut.
One sandal.
Ninety-two people were on that flight.
She calls for her mother.
Nobody answers.
Then she hears water.
And she remembers her father's rule.
Find moving water.
Never let it out of your sight.
The stream becomes a brook.
The brook becomes a river.
Vultures circle where the bodies are.
Maggots eat into her arm.
On the tenth day,
she finds a boat.
She pours its fuel into the wound.
On the eleventh,
she hears men's voices.
Like the voices of angels.
Ninety-one people died.
One walked out.
Her full story is on No Rescue.
