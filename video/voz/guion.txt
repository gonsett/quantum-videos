## gancho
A diver reaches for a dead man's hand, a hundred feet down.
And the hand grabs him back.
That man had been down there for sixty hours, in the dark.

## the tug
May 2013, off Nigeria. A tug called the Jascon-4 rolls over before dawn, with twelve men on board.
The sea carries the cook, Harrison Okene, into a tiny washroom, as she sinks upside down.
And when his head comes up, there is air. A pocket four feet high.

## the dark
His flashlights die within a day. He promises God he will never go back to the sea.
On the surface, nobody is looking for survivors. The divers have come for the bodies.

## the hand
Then, through the steel, he hears hammering. He reaches a diver's light, and takes his hand.
He's alive, says a voice on the line.
He came up. And he went back to the sea. His full story is on No Rescue.
