## gancho
This country is smaller than Central Park. And by some measures, it's the richest place on Earth.
No airport. No farmland. Almost no natural resources. And its residents pay no income tax.
## perla
In 1861, its prince sold most of the country to France. For four million francs.
With the cash, his family built a casino. Monte Carlo.
The money came in so fast that, in 1869, the prince abolished the direct tax on his own people.
The twist? Monaco's own citizens are still not allowed to gamble in it.
## rescate
Today, the real product is the tax deal. The state's biggest source of money is the tax on what people buy. Yachts. Watches. Apartments.
A square meter costs more than fifty thousand euros. So when Monaco ran out of land, it built more. Out of the sea.
## giro
And the people who keep it running? Nearly nine out of ten live somewhere else. They cross the border every morning.
Monaco isn't a country that happens to be rich. It's a business that happens to be a country.
