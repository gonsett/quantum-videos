## gancho
The longest road on Earth is missing a hundred kilometers.
The Pan-American Highway runs about thirty thousand kilometers, from Alaska to the bottom of Argentina. Except here.
So why has nobody ever finished it?
## perla
In Panama, the road stops at a small town called Yaviza.
It starts again in Colombia, near the Gulf of Urabá. In between, swamp, mountains and rainforest that no road has ever crossed.
## rescate
In 1960, a team tried it with a Land Rover and a Jeep. The crossing took them more than four months.
In 1971, the United States agreed to pay two thirds of the cost of closing the gap. The bulldozers moved in.
Then, in 1975, a federal judge in Washington ordered the work to stop.
## giro
Part of the reason was cows. South America had foot-and-mouth disease, and the only thing standing between it and the herds up north was the Darién.
The road never came. The people did. In 2023, more than five hundred and twenty thousand walked through it. On foot.
About one in five was a child.
